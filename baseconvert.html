Frequently asked questions

What number bases does this converter support?

Binary (base 2), octal (base 8), decimal (base 10) and hexadecimal (base 16). Enter a value in any one base and all four are shown at once. Hexadecimal letters A–F can be typed in upper or lower case.

How large a number can I convert?

Very large. The converter uses arbitrary-precision arithmetic, so numbers far beyond the usual 64-bit range — for example a 256-bit hash value — convert exactly, with no rounding.

Why does my input show an error?

Each base allows only certain digits: binary uses 0–1, octal 0–7, decimal 0–9, and hexadecimal 0–9 plus A–F. A digit outside the selected base, such as a 2 in a binary number, is rejected. Negative numbers and spaces after digits are accepted.

Is this converter free and private?

Yes. It is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
