Roman numerals use subtractive pairs for 4s and 9s — IV (4), IX (9), XL (40), XC (90), CD (400) and CM (900) — and a symbol is never repeated more than three times. That is why the standard system tops out at 3999 (MMMCMXCIX): there is no symbol above M in everyday use. The converter back checks that a numeral follows these rules, so non-standard forms are flagged instead of silently converted.
