Frequently asked questions

How do you write 1,250 in words?

1,250 is written “one thousand two hundred fifty”. On a check you would typically write “One thousand two hundred fifty and 00/100” in the amount line.

How large a number can this convert?

Whole numbers up to just under one quadrillion (999,999,999,999,999), including negatives, plus decimals — the part after the decimal point is read digit by digit after the word “point”, so 3.14 becomes “three point one four”.

Does it use “and” in numbers like 105?

This converter follows the common American convention and omits “and”: 105 is “one hundred five”. British usage often writes “one hundred and five” — both are understood, but follow your document's style.

Is this converter free and private?

Yes. It is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
