Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the four reverse modes?

Reverse text flips every character, so “hello world” becomes “dlrow olleh”. Reverse word order keeps each word intact but flips their order: “world hello”. Reverse each word flips the letters inside every word while the order stays: “olleh dlrow”. Reverse lines flips the order of lines in multi-line text, keeping each line as it is.

Does it work with emoji and accented characters?

Yes. Characters are reversed by Unicode code point rather than by raw bytes or UTF-16 units, so emoji and accented letters are never split into broken halves. Very complex emoji made of several joined characters (such as family emoji) may separate into their individual parts when flipped, which is normal for character-level reversal.

What do people use reversed text for?

Common uses include checking palindromes, mirror-writing tricks for social posts and puzzles, reversing lists and log lines, flipping word order when editing, and playful backwards messages. It is also a quick way to spot-check that a string reads the same from both ends.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The reversal runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Your text never leaves your device.
