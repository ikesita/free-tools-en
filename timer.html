Frequently asked questions

How long can I set the timer for?

Up to 99 hours, 59 minutes and 59 seconds. Enter any combination of hours, minutes and seconds, or tap a preset such as 5 minutes or 25 minutes (a Pomodoro).

Will the timer still ring if I switch tabs?

Yes. The timer measures the real end time rather than counting ticks, so it stays accurate in a background tab. The alert sound plays when the countdown finishes — keep the tab open and your sound on.

How is this different from the Days Until Calculator?

This timer counts down hours, minutes and seconds — for cooking, workouts or focused work. The Days Until Calculator counts whole days between calendar dates.

Is this timer free and private?

Yes. It is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.

Your input stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
