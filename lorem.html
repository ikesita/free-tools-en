Frequently asked questions

Where does Lorem Ipsum come from?

It is scrambled Latin based on Cicero's “de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum”, written in 45 BC. Printers have used it as dummy text since the 1500s because it looks like natural text without distracting the reader with real content.

What does “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet” mean?

Strictly, nothing — the words were scrambled and shortened over the centuries. The original passage concerned pain and pleasure (“dolorem ipsum”, “pain itself”), so it is best treated as neutral filler, not a real translation.
