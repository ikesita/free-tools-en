How to convert liters to gallons (us)

Formula: Gallons = Liters ÷ 3.785411784.

This converter uses the US liquid gallon (3.785411784 liters). The UK (imperial) gallon is larger, at 4.54609 liters.

Example: 5 L × the factor above = 1.3209 gal.
