How old is your cat in human years? Cats mature fast in their first two years, then age about 4 human years per cat year.

This uses the widely used veterinary rule of thumb, not the old "multiply by 7": a cat's first year ≈ 15 human years, the second year adds about 9 (24 total), and each year after that adds about 4. For example, a 3-year-old cat is about 28 in human years, and a 10-year-old cat is about 56.
