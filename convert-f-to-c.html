How to convert fahrenheit to celsius

Formula: Celsius = (Fahrenheit − 32) × 5/9.

Normal body temperature is about 98.6 °F, which is 37 °C. Water freezes at 32 °F (0 °C).

Example: 77 °F × the factor above = 25 °C.
