Frequently asked questions

Is this coin flip fair?

Yes. Each flip uses your browser's secure random number generator, and every flip is independent — heads and tails each have a 50% chance, no matter what came before.

How many coins can I flip at once?

You can flip between 1 and 100 coins at a time. The page shows each coin, the heads and tails totals for that flip, and a running tally for the whole session.

Does flipping heads five times in a row change the next flip?

No. That idea is called the gambler's fallacy. Every flip is independent, so the chance of heads on the next flip is still 50%.

Is this coin flip free and private?

Yes. It is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
