How to convert celsius to fahrenheit

Formula: Fahrenheit = Celsius × 9/5 + 32.

Water freezes at 0 °C (32 °F) and boils at 100 °C (212 °F). The two scales meet at −40°.

Example: 25 °C × the factor above = 77 °F.
