Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalize Each Word?

On this page they are the same conversion: the first letter of every word is capitalized and the rest are lowercased. Some style guides (like AP) keep short words such as “the” and “of” lowercase in titles — check your guide if that matters.

What are camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case used for?

They are naming conventions for code and URLs. camelCase joins words with each new word capitalized (myVariableName), snake_case separates words with underscores (my_variable_name), and kebab-case uses hyphens (my-variable-name), which is common in web addresses.

Does the converter change my original text?

The conversion replaces the text in the box, so copy anything you want to keep first — or convert, copy the result, and use your browser's undo (Ctrl+Z) to step back. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Is this converter free and private?

Yes. It is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — your text is never sent to a server.
