Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this stopwatch?

It reads the system clock directly instead of counting screen refreshes, so the displayed time stays accurate to the hundredth of a second shown, even if the display updates less often in a background tab.

What is the difference between lap and split times?

A split time is the total elapsed time when you press Lap. The lap time is how long that single lap took — the difference between two splits. This stopwatch shows both, and highlights your fastest and slowest laps.

Does the stopwatch keep running in another tab?

Yes. Because it is based on the clock rather than a counter, switching tabs does not lose time. Come back and the correct elapsed time is shown.

Is this stopwatch free and private?

Yes. It is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.

Your input stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
