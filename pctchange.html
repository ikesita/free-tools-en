Percentage Change Calculator

The increase or decrease from one value to another, as a number and as a percentage.

Percentage change-

Percentage change is always relative to the original (from) value — that is why going from 80 to 100 is +25%, but going back from 100 to 80 is −20%. A percentage change cannot be calculated from an original value of 0.
