How to convert kilograms to pounds

Formula: Pounds = Kilograms × 2.20462.

One kilogram is defined as exactly 2.2046226218 pounds (since 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg exactly).

Example: 70 kg × the factor above = 154.3236 lb.
