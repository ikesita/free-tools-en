Frequently asked questions

How is the CSV converted to JSON?

The first row of the CSV becomes the keys, and each following row becomes one JSON object in an array. All values stay as strings, because CSV itself has no number or boolean types — convert them in your code if you need typed values.

Are quoted fields and line breaks inside cells supported?

Yes. The parser follows RFC 4180: fields wrapped in double quotes may contain the delimiter, line breaks, and double quotes written as two double quotes (“”). Files exported from Excel and Google Sheets parse correctly.

Which delimiters are supported?

Comma, semicolon and tab. Choose the one your file uses — semicolon is common in European Excel exports, and tab matches TSV files pasted from spreadsheets.

Is my CSV uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device, which makes the tool safe for exports containing personal or business data.
